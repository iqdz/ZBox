Uhlu lwemilayezo
U-Delete, Shift+Delete, Gcina, Maka ngenkanyezi/Susa inkanyezi, Maka njengokungafuneki/Njengokufunekayo, Hambisa ku- no-Kopisha ku- konke kusebenza emilayezweni yonke ekuntambo uma kusetshenziswa emgqeni wentambo OGOQIWE -- vula intambo kuqala (Right arrow noma Enter/Space) ukuze wenze ngomlayezo owodwa ngaphakathi kwayo
Ctrl+A: Khetha yonke imilayezo
Ctrl+Shift+L: Layisha imilayezo eminye
Delete: Hambisa imilayezo kudoti
Shift+Delete: Susa imilayezo unomphela
Ctrl+Z: Hlehlisa u-Susa, Gcina, Maka njengokungafuneki noma Maka njengokufunekayo wokugcina
S: Maka ngenkanyezi/susa inkanyezi emilayezweni ekhethiwe
A: Gcina imilayezo ekhethiwe
J: Maka imilayezo ekhethiwe njengokungafuneki
Shift+J: Maka imilayezo ekhethiwe njengokufunekayo
W: Bheka intambo -- shintsha ukuthi intambo okuyingxenye yayo ukukhetha iyabhekwa yini (nakumenyu Yomlayezo nokuchofoza kwesokudla)
K: Ziba intambo -- shintsha ukuthi leyo ntambo iyazibwa yini (nakumenyu Yomlayezo nokuchofoza kwesokudla)
M: Hambisa imilayezo ekhethiwe -- ivula imenyu ethi Hambisa ku- yamafolda
Shift+M: Hambisa imilayezo ekhethiwe ngokuthayipha ingxenye yegama lefolda
C: Kopisha imilayezo ekhethiwe -- ivula imenyu ethi Kopisha ku- yamafolda
U-S, A, J, W no-K abangenhla bavuliwe ngokuzenzakalelayo futhi bangavalwa ku-Amasethingi (bathola ukuza kuqala kunokuhamba kohlu lwemilayezo ngohlamvu lokuqala)
U-M, Shift+M no-C abangenhla balandela isethingi esifanayo
Enter noma Space entambeni egoqiwe: ivula umlayezo omusha ongafundiwe kule ntambo (umlayezo omusha uma yonke intambo ifundiwe) njengomlayezo owodwa, kuthebhu eyodwa -- bese u-Ctrl+Shift+Page Down no-Ctrl+Shift+Page Up bedlulisa ingxenye esele yentambo ngaphandle kokuvula umlayezo wesibili. Emlayezweni ovamile, uyivula njengokujwayelekile
Right arrow entambeni egoqiwe: ikhombisa imilayezo yayo njengohlu, ngaphandle kokuvula noma yimuphi -- vula owodwa ngesikhathi kusukela lapho ngo-Enter noma Space wawo
Ctrl+Shift+O: Vula umlayezo embhulweni -- yonke intambo kuthebhu eyodwa, njengohlu lwemilayezo yayo nomzimba wokhethiwe ngaphansi. Hamba ngemicibisholo ohlwini ukuze uzifunde ngazinye; u-Enter emgqeni uvula lowo mlayezo owodwa kuthebhu yawo egcwele. Igama nokhiye ofanayo nomyalo we-Thunderbird uqobo
Left arrow: goqa intambo lapho ukukhetha kuseyingxenye yayo
*: Vula zonke izintambo (nango-Buka > Izintambo); \: Goqa zonke izintambo (nango-Buka > Izintambo)
U-Buka > Izintambo > Zonke / Izintambo ezibhekiwe / Izintambo ezizibiwe uhlunga ukuthi iziphi izintambo ezikhonjiswayo -- ukubuyela ku-Zonke kuhlala kubuyisa yonke imilayezo; akukho okususwa efolda ngalokhu

Umlayezo ovuliwe
Alt+A: Gcina lo mlayezo -- kuphela uma umzimba wawo (Umbhalo noma i-HTML) unefokhasi yekhibhodi ngempela; kwenye indawo u-Alt+A ugcina incazelo yawo evamile yokuvula imenyu ethi Ama-akhawunti
Ctrl+R: Phendula
Ctrl+Shift+R: Phendula bonke
Ctrl+L: Dlulisela
Ctrl+Delete noma Ctrl+D: Hambisa lo mlayezo kudoti. U-Delete ovamile uvame ukuthathwa ngumfundi wesikrini ngenkathi umlayezo we-HTML unefokhasi; u-Shift+Delete usula unomphela
Ctrl+Shift+Page Down: Umlayezo olandelayo kule ntambo; Ctrl+Shift+Page Up: odlule. Ushintsha ithebhu yamanje esikhundleni sokuvula enye, ngakho umlayezo owodwa kuphela uhlala uvuliwe. Emlayezweni wokugcina (noma wokuqala, uma ukhuphuka) uyasho futhi uyabuza; ukucindezela ukhiye ofanayo futhi kuvala umlayezo futhi kuqede intambo, kubuyela ohlwini lwemilayezo. (U-Ctrl+Page Up/Down uyashiywa -- lawo ashintsha amathebhu)
